- **Overhang check (`mesh_tool.py overhang`) and resolution of the 3-to-2 support discrepancy**:
  - **Cause of the previous 3-to-2 difference**:
    1. *Geometry*: The **three variants with a `+X` hood cap** (`abschluss-links`, `mitte-beidseitig`, `10cm-rechts`) had only `2,30 - 1,57 = 0,73 mm`-wide first-layer contact strips on `Z = 0,00 mm` with `Y_SADDLE_SPINE = 1,45 mm` (`y_spine_hood = 1,57 mm`)—less than two extrusion widths, leaving just one first-layer contact point. The **two variants with a `-X` snap-on saddle** (`mitte-beidseitig`, `abschluss-rechts`) instead had a `39,2°` overhang slope (`nz = -0,775`) at the saddle tip.
    2. *3MF process metadata*: Process overrides in `Metadata/project_settings.config` were incorrectly stored in `different_settings_to_system[1]` instead of `different_settings_to_system[0]`; the file also used `version = 02.06.00.51` and 93 new OrcaSlicer 2.4.2 keys. AnycubicSlicerNext `2.0.0.3` therefore discarded the process overrides (`seam_position=random`, `ironing_type=top`, `support_threshold_angle=30`, `support_critical_regions_only=1`) when opening the file, and `SupportSpotsGenerator` generated supports for the three objects with `<metadata key="enable_support" value="1"/>`.
  - **Resolution**:
    1. `Y_SADDLE_SPINE = 1,15 mm` (`y_spine_hood = 1,27 mm`) widens the first-layer contact strips of the `+X` hood cap to **`1,03 mm` per side** (at least two full extrusion lines). `Z_SADDLE_SHOULDER = 1,68 mm` + `1,10 * delta_y_key` and the `±0,42 / ±0,48 mm` snap-bead ramps raise all connector overhangs to **`>= 50,8° .. 69,4°` from horizontal** (`<= 39,2°` from vertical; entire part `<= 45,0°` from vertical).
    2. `different_settings_to_system[0]` now stores all 23 process overrides (`seam_position=random`, `ironing_type=top` ["All top surfaces"], `layer_height=0.16`, `enable_support=1`, `support_type=tree(auto)`, etc.) using the `2.0.0.3`-compatible schema (`print_settings_id = "0.16mm Standard @Anycubic Kobra S1 0.4 nozzle"`).
  - `unsupported area (> 45° from vertical)`: **`0,00 mm²` (`0,0 %`)** on all five variants.
  - `ceilings (>= 89°)`: **`0,00 mm²`** (`max ceiling short span = 0,00 mm`, `PASS`).

## 11. Assembly Order (Verified in Sections 5/6)

1. Place the chest (chest) on the table, opening upward.
2. Press the slider (slider) vertically onto the rail until both snap fingers engage in the groove.
3. Slide the drive pinion axially through the front-wall hole from inside (the rack meshes).
4. Fit the skull knob onto the D-shaft; insert a Ø1.75 x 14 filament pin crosswise through knob and shaft.
5. Lower the lid follower into the guide channel; press the follower pin from inside through the follower into the cam slot.
6. Insert both lid transfer gears axially (-X) into the bearing blocks, each with a Ø1.75 x 5 filament pin crosswise through the journal from outside.
7. Install the scissor arms in each layer; snap in the floor, slider, and center pins from inside (heads inward).
8. Place the scissor drive gear onto the upper arm eye from +Y; press the locating pin into the arm hole (before the platform!).
9. Preassemble the platform assembly outside the chest: forearm between the ears, output-gear D-shaft through, Ø1.75 x 10 filament retainer pin, three idler gears with idler pins. Lower vertically, offset 3 mm away from the scissor drive gear (toward idler gear 0), then slide into engagement along the axis connection; snap the gear/fixed/slot pins into the top joints.
10. Fit the hand onto the forearm pin; insert a Ø1.75 x 16 filament pin crosswise.
11. Preassemble the lid assembly outside the chest: turn the lid upside down, insert the lid crank from below and slide it in +X into the drive pocket; place the spacer from below into the service channel between crank and lid eye.
12. Hold the lid assembly open ~108 degrees, lower vertically with a 3 mm rearward (+Y) offset, then slide forward into engagement with the idler gear until the hinge eyes align; slide Ø1.75 x 198 hinge filament through chest, lid, crank, and spacer.
